## 1. Executive summary: the ten things that matter

0. **Too many doors for the day's work.** Nineteen sidebar entries and thirteen admin tabs for a product whose usage is My Day, the boards, the calendar and setup. Section 4.0 sets the target: eight doors, four settings groups, five pages retired or merged. (N-01 to N-07, B-08)
1. **Three ways to create a task, three visual languages.** "New task" opens the quick idea form (`QuickTask.jsx`); "More fields" opens the step builder (`builder/TaskBuilder.jsx`); "All fields" on the builder, and **Edit on every task window**, open the legacy full sheet (`ContentModal.jsx`, 2 421 lines) with native `mm/dd/yyyy` date inputs, a "— pick a format —" placeholder that is untranslated, an English hint in the Russian UI, chips that render as "…" until data arrives, and a layout that jumps as fields load. The person who edits a task they just created meets a different product. (T-01 to T-06)
2. **Ruby is spent on the wrong thing.** The brand says ruby is for the one button to press, where you are, and what is late. On every page the loudest element is the sidebar's "New task" button, even on pages where creating is not the job (Statistics, Profile, Admin). On a maker's My Day the start-here card is a ruby-tinted block with a ruby button inside it, and below it three more ruby late badges and outlined buttons. There is no single primary. (S-03, D-02)
3. **Type and spacing drift.** The stylesheet uses 10.5, 11, 11.5, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 14.5 px as body sizes (over a thousand declarations between 11 and 14.5 px), fifteen different corner radii, 191 `box-shadow` rules and eight gradients. The brand says no gradients and no shadows at rest. Nothing looks broken, everything looks slightly different. (S-01, S-02)
4. **The admin's My Day is a different product from everybody else's.** Every other person gets "{name}, start here." and a big card; the admin gets a Gantt of people with hour columns, a hatched "day is over" region, five filters and a legend of six symbols. On a phone it is a scrollable grid with the "now" line off screen. The first-run tour tells the admin to "start at the top with the big card" which the admin's page does not have. (D-01, D-05)
5. **The next-step question is a centred modal, not a panel at the card.** `CLAUDE.md` says the board asks "one question at a time in a small panel at the card". It is a 820 px modal with a seven-day row, four duration chips and a 9×3 grid of green time buttons per operator (27 choices before "+ ещё 9"). Inside the task window the same question is squeezed into a 380 px column and wraps into four rows. (T-07, T-08)
6. **Names do not match doors.** "Calendar" and "Shoots" are the same page (`Schedule.jsx`) with a mode; "Statistics" lives at `/missed`, "Payment" at `/docs` and shows a "Monthly Delivery Report"; "Post Production" is the crew capacity page at `/crew`; "Team & hiring" and Admin → Team both list the same twelve people; Overview exists at `/overview` in nobody's menu. (N-01 to N-05)
7. **Untranslated and inconsistent words.** "To shoot" on the Russian phone calendar, "— pick a format —", "Short enough that whoever picks this up will have to ask", "Lectures" in the Russian admin grid. Dates appear as "пт 6 нояб.", "2026-11-06", "11/06/2026" and "Fri 6 Nov" on the same task. (S-05, S-06)
8. **Accessibility gaps that also hurt sighted users.** The builder's step tabs, kind radios and person buttons have no accessible names; eleven `outline: none` rules with no replacement; folded content in the quick idea form is in the accessibility tree while hidden; wheel scrolling inside the full sheet scrolls the page behind it; the task window opens scrolled past its own title after a prior scroll. (S-07, T-09)
9. **One failed request empties the product.** When `/api/channels` fails once (it did, after a server restart), the sidebar has no channels, the new-idea dialog shows "Where does it go?" with no options, and nothing says why or offers a retry. The channels provider trusts the first answer. (S-08)
10. **Empty and secondary pages explain nothing.** Sprints opens on a frozen empty sprint with four empty columns; Projects shows "no deadline" three times with "On Track" badges; Ambassadors is an empty month; Uploads is a one-line setup notice; Payment for an admin is three cards with nothing in them. These are the pages the owner opens on a desk. (A-01 to A-08)

## 3. System-level tasks (S)

### S-01 · Type scale tokens · P0 · M
**Seen.** `styles.css` sets body sizes at 10.5, 11, 11.5, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 14.5, 15, 15.5, 16 px. Card titles are 13 px on the board, 14.5 px in a My Day row, 15 px on a crew card, 16 px in the task window. Labels are 11 px uppercase tracked in Admin, 12 px sentence case on My Day, 12.5 px in the task window's "ТЗ / SCRIPT / DESCRIPTION" captions.
**Problem.** No hierarchy step is at least 1.25× its neighbour, so weight is carried by boldness and colour instead; half-pixel sizes render differently per browser; the same thing (a task's title) has four sizes.
**Do.** Add tokens at the top of `styles.css`: `--fs-xs: 12px`, `--fs-sm: 13px`, `--fs-md: 15px` (body), `--fs-lg: 17px`, `--fs-xl: 20px`, `--fs-2xl: 24px`, `--fs-display: 32px` (Nekst, the page headline and big numbers only) and line heights `--lh-tight: 1.2`, `--lh-body: 1.45`. Replace every `font-size` in the `r8`, `r10` and `co-` sections with a token; everything 10.5 to 11.5 px becomes `--fs-xs`; 12 to 13.5 becomes `--fs-sm`; 14 to 15.5 becomes `--fs-md`. Leave the previous look's sections alone. Small uppercase tracked labels (`.k-label`, section eyebrows in Admin and Team) become sentence case at `--fs-sm`, `--muted`.
**Accept.** `grep -cE "font-size:\s*[0-9]+(\.[0-9])?px" styles.css` inside the Coach sections is zero; a title reads as one size on My Day, the board, the calendar chip and the task window; no uppercase tracked label remains in the Coach look. Extend `qa/brand-suite.mjs` to fail on a raw `font-size` px value in a `co-`, `r8` or `r10` rule.

### S-02 · Radius, shadow and gradient tokens · P0 · S
**Seen.** Fifteen radius values (3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16, 999 px and five tokens); 191 `box-shadow` rules; eight `gradient(` uses (two are masks and may stay, the Gantt stripes and the whiteboard dots are decoration).
**Do.** Four radii only: `--radius-xs: 6px` (chips), `--radius-sm: 10px` (buttons, inputs), `--radius: 14px` (cards, panels), `--radius-pill: 999px`. Shadows only on what floats (menus, the search box, the next-step panel, toasts, the uploads dock); every card, row and tile at rest separates by the surface step (`--surface` on `--page`), never by a shadow. Remove the gradient on the Gantt bars and the whiteboard's dotted ground (use a radial dot pattern drawn with `background-image: radial-gradient` is still a gradient under the brand rule; replace with an SVG dot tile in `client/public/`).
**Accept.** `qa/brand-suite.mjs` fails on any `gradient(` outside `mask-image`, and on `box-shadow` in a rule whose selector is not in an allow-list of floating pieces.

### S-03 · One primary per view · P0 · M
**Seen.** The sidebar's "New task" (`co-give`, ruby fill) is the heaviest element on every page. On a maker's My Day the start-here card has a ruby band, a ruby tint and a ruby button; the overdue rows carry ruby badges; every row has an outlined button; the crew's "Send to Dilnoza" is ruby while a designer's disabled "Send to Javohir" is a pink wash. On the board every card has an outlined button of the same weight, and "New idea" is ruby in the toolbar.
**Do.**
- Sidebar: "New task" becomes a tonal button (`--primary-soft` family, near-black text, plus icon). Ruby in the sidebar is reserved for the active row's text and the late counts.
- My Day start-here card: the card is white on the grey page with a ruby left rule only (no tint); the one ruby button is the card's. All list rows below use the tonal button style (`.co-btn-s`), and ruby appears only on a late badge.
- Board: card buttons tonal; "New idea" tonal with a plus; the one ruby element on a board is the "Publish" button on a Ready card, because publishing is the board's finish line.
- Task window: the step box's button is the only ruby; "Edit" and the close button are quiet; the stages line's current stop is near-black, not ruby.
**Accept.** On every captured screen at most one ruby-filled button is visible without scrolling. Add a check to `qa/coach-suite.mjs` that renders My Day for each demo account and counts `.co-btn-p:visible` ≤ 1 above the fold.

### S-04 · Spacing scale and card rhythm · P1 · M
**Seen.** Padding inside Coach cards ranges 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22 px; the gap between My Day sections is 24 px while the gap inside a section between rows is 0 with hairlines; Admin tabs use 16 px; the board column padding is 10 px and the card padding 14 px, so a card's inside is wider than its outside.
**Do.** `--sp-1: 4px` … `--sp-6: 24px`, `--sp-8: 32px`, `--sp-12: 48px`. Rule: a container's inner padding ≤ the gap between it and its neighbour. Cards: 16 px inside, 12 px between; sections: 32 px between on a desk, 24 px on a phone; page gutter 32 px desk, 16 px phone. Replace values in the `co-`, `r8`, `r10` sections.
**Accept.** No raw `padding`/`gap`/`margin` px in those sections except 0 and 1.
**Added (1 November 2026):** `--sp-0: 2px` is on the scale — the hairline gap between a mark and its word (a tab's two pills, a row's icon and its title), which the look used in about ninety places and which 0 would close and 4 would open.

### S-05 · Untranslated and mixed-language strings · P0 · S
**Seen.** "To shoot" on the Russian phone calendar chip; "— pick a format —" in `ContentModal.jsx:1315`; "Short enough that whoever picks this up will have to ask." in the Russian edit form; "Lectures" in the admin's day grid; "Monthly Delivery Report", "KPI document" on the Russian Payment page (check); stage names fall back to English in several chips.
**Do.** Run a scan: every JSX text node and every `title`/`aria-label`/`placeholder` not wrapped in `tx()` or `t()` is listed; wrap them; add the missing lines in all three languages in `lib/i18n.jsx`. Stage names on chips always go through the status's localised label. Add `qa/i18n-suite.mjs`: for `ru` and `uz`, render each demo account's My Day, board, calendar, task window and the three task forms with Playwright and fail on a visible string matching a list of known English words (`shoot`, `edit`, `pick`, `format`, `lectures`, `ready`, `published`, `today`).
**Accept.** The suite passes in `ru` and `uz`.

### S-06 · One date and time format · P0 · S
**Seen.** The same task shows "пт 6 нояб." on a row, "2026-11-06" in More details → Deadlines, "11/06/2026" in Edit, "Fri 6 Nov" in English; the Russian builder shows "пн 19 окт." and "Октябрь – Ноябрь 2026". Replace native date inputs in the Coach look with the builder's `MiniCalendar.jsx` as a popover (one component, `components/DayPick.jsx`), and native time inputs with the half-hour rows from `TimeBand.jsx`. The legacy sheet keeps native inputs until T-05 retires it.
**Accept.** A task's day reads identically in the row, the window, the plan column, the calendar chip and the builder.

### S-07 · Accessibility floor · P0 · M
**Seen.** In the builder, the four step tabs are unnamed buttons; the three kind cards are unnamed radios; the person pickers are unnamed buttons. `outline: none` without a visible replacement at `styles.css:662, 1365, 3116, 4072, 4119, 4683, 7358, 7813, 9374, 10402`. Folds render nothing while shut (check `Fold.jsx`; make `QuickTask.jsx`'s "I already know more" fold use it or unmount its content). `overscroll-behavior: contain` on every `.modal`, `.co-panel`, `.co-ask`, `.mob-sheet`. `role="status"` on the toast host; `aria-live="polite"` on the uploads dock. Touch targets 44 px on the phone for row buttons, chips that act as filters, and the stage chips on the phone board.
**Accept.** Add `qa/a11y-suite.mjs` running axe-core (devDependency) over the same screens as S-05 and failing on serious and critical findings; keyboard-only walk: Tab reaches every control on My Day, the task window and the builder, Escape closes every layer in order.

### S-08 · Resilience of the shell · P0 · S
**Seen.** After one failed `GET /api/channels` the sidebar drew no channels, "New idea" offered no channel, and nothing said why. The provider in `lib/channels.jsx` boots from cache and refreshes once.
**Do.** On a failed first load: retry with back-off (1 s, 3 s, 10 s), and while there are no channels draw a quiet line in the sidebar's channel group ("Channels did not load. Retrying…" with a Retry button) and in the new-idea dialog in place of the empty grid. Same for `/fields` (page rules) and `/users`. Never draw an empty picker without a sentence.
**Accept.** Kill the API, load the page, restart the API: channels appear within 10 s without a reload; the dialog never shows an empty "Where does it go?".

### S-09 · Button and chip vocabulary · P1 · M
**Seen.** Buttons exist as `.btn`, `.co-btn`, `.co-btn-p`, `.co-btn-s`, `.co-btn-sm`, `.tchip`, `.chip`, pill tabs (`.seg`), outlined pill (Calendar "Filter"), filled pill ("Hand it back" on the design board), tonal pill ("Has capacity"), square-cornered (Admin "Add member") and fully round (phone bar). **Do.** Three button kinds only, documented at the top of the `co-` section: primary (ruby fill, light-grey text), tonal (`--primary-soft` fill, near-black text), quiet (no fill, near-black text, underline on hover). One shape: `--radius-sm`, 40 px tall on a desk, 44 px on a phone; the phone bar's round plus is the one exception. Chips: a **tag** (grey fill, sentence case, for type and channel), a **status** (dot + word, no fill, for stage), a **badge** (ruby tint + ruby text, only for late / count of late), a **person** (avatar + first name). No filled coloured chips except the type tag on the board card, which keeps its type colour at 13 % tint with the type colour as text (like the Coach kind tiles). Migrate `Design.jsx`, `Crew.jsx`, `Team.jsx`, `Admin.jsx` tabs, `Schedule.jsx` chips.
**Accept.** A screenshot of the board, the design board, Post Production and Admin → Channels shows one chip grammar.

### S-10 · Dialog and layer grammar · P1 · M
**Seen.** Ten components declare `role="dialog"`; layers close differently (×, "Закрыть", Escape, click outside, "Cancel", "Not now"); the task window is a right drawer on a desk and a full sheet on a phone; the next-step question is a centred modal from the board but inline in the window; the quick idea form, the builder and the full sheet are three centred modals of three widths (440, 520, 860 px); the notifications popover is anchored top-right; search floats top-centre.

Implement in `Modal.jsx` with a `kind` prop and migrate the ten.
**Accept.** `qa/coach-suite.mjs` opens each layer on the demo and checks: one scrim, Escape order, focus return.
